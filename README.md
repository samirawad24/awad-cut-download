# Awad Cut

A video editor for Windows. Cut, caption, color and export your videos, all on your own computer.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/samirawad24/awad-cut-download/releases/latest)**, then click `Awad-Cut-Setup-<version>.exe` under Assets.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

## Install

1. Open the downloaded file.
2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
   The app is safe; the warning appears because it is not code signed yet.
3. If your browser says the file "isn't commonly downloaded", choose **Keep**.
4. Pick a folder and finish the installer. Awad Cut is added to your desktop and Start menu.

## What it does

- Timeline editing with multiple video, audio and title tracks
- Smart Cut: remove silences, split at scene changes, duck music under voice, beat markers
- Auto Captions with word-by-word highlight styles (needs internet once to download the speech model)
- Transcript editing: delete words in the text to cut them from the video
- Reformat for Social (9:16, 4:5, 1:1) with Auto Reframe that keeps faces in frame
- Enhance Voice: removes background noise and hum, evens out volume
- LUT color looks, including camera log conversions, and your own .cube files
- Export to MP4 (H.264 or HEVC), ProRes, WebM, GIF, MP3 and WAV
